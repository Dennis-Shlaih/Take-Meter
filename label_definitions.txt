analysis: the author's own claim is backed by a relevant, checkable detail stated in the post text: for example a number tied to a stated setup, an exact prompt or input used in a described test, a model/quant/hardware configuration relevant to a comparison, a calculation, or a linked source whose finding the post states; bare links, screenshots, and demos alone do not count; a vague personal impression without those details is hot_take
announcement: primarily shares a release, news item, result, or technical resource, including the author's own tool, model, project, tutorial, update, or invitation to the author's own group/community, without making a separate evidence-backed claim the post is chiefly defending; if the author adds their own check, comparison, calculation, or critique, use analysis
hot_take: the author argues or asserts a judgment about the field, a tool, a model, a person, or other people without relevant, checkable support stated in the post text; this includes personal impressions without such details and insults aimed at a tool or person; anger, profanity, and unsupported or unverifiable figures do not make it analysis
reaction: the post's content is mainly the author's feeling about a specific moment or event, or a personal experience, with no general quality judgment being argued; a general judgment such as a tool or model being "the dumbest" is hot_take
