analysis: the author's own claim is backed by at least one checkable detail given in the post (a measurement with hardware and config, a comparison across sources, a calculation, a flagged inconsistency)
announcement: relays a release, news item, or someone else's results, with no claim of the author's own to defend
hot_take: the author argues or asserts a judgment about the field, a tool, or other people, without support a reader could check; anger, profanity and unverifiable figures do not make it analysis
reaction: the post's content is mainly the author's feeling (excitement, amusement, frustration), with no judgment being argued
